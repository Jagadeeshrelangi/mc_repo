## 1. Revenue Model

| Stream | Model | Margin | Timeline |
|--------|-------|--------|----------|
| Mechanic Booking | 15–20% commission per transaction | ₹60–90/booking | Sprint 2 |
| Fuel Delivery | Delivery fee + fuel margin | ₹20–50/delivery | Sprint 1.7 |
| Marketplace Listing | Monthly subscription for sellers | ₹99/seller/month | Sprint 1.8 |
| Featured Mechanics | Priority placement fee | ₹499/mechanic/month | Sprint 2 |
| AI Diagnosis API | B2B subscription | ₹999/fleet/month | Sprint 3 |
| In-app Ads | Sponsored listings | Variable | Sprint 3 |

## 4. Growth Strategy

| Phase | Timeline | Focus |
|-------|----------|-------|
| Seed | Sprint 1–2 | User acquisition in 1 city |
| Series A | Sprint 3–4 | Expand to 5 cities |
| Growth | Sprint 5+ | Partner app, B2B fleet |

### Acquisition Channels
- Google Ads (keyword: "mechanic near me")
- Instagram/Facebook (target: vehicle owners)
- Referral program (₹50 credit per referral)
- Partnership with parking lots/service stations
